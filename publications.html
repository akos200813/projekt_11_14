Yale School of Art Publications

Explore a selection of scholarly and creative publications from the Yale School of Art. Dive into the latest research, student work, and exhibitions through our publications.

Yale Art Journal

A leading journal showcasing contemporary art, including articles, interviews, and insights from both faculty and students.

Art and Architecture Review

A comprehensive review of the intersection between art and architecture at Yale, featuring innovative works and critical discussions.

Yale Art History Review

The latest edition of the Yale Art History Review, featuring scholarly articles on art history, theory, and practices.

MFA Thesis Exhibition Catalog

A catalog of the annual MFA Thesis Exhibition, showcasing the creative work of graduating students in the art program.

Undergraduate Exhibition Catalog

An exhibition catalog featuring works from the annual undergraduate show at Yale School of Art, celebrating emerging talents.

Yale Design Annual

A publication highlighting the most exceptional design works from students and faculty at the Yale School of Art.

Alumni Portfolio Review

A review that highlights the best works from alumni portfolios, celebrating the lasting impact of Yale-trained artists on the field.
