Exhibitions

The School of Art galleries provide a year-round forum for exhibitions by students, faculty, and invited guests.

The Yale School of Art galleries in Green Hall and 32 Edgewood Avenue support a dynamic exhibition program across the School’s graduate departments and undergraduate program.

Exhibitions are generally open to the Yale community. Public access is available during scheduled receptions.

Current Exhibition

A Grand Union

32 Edgewood Gallery
December 2 – December 8

Public reception: Wednesday, December 3, 5:30–8:00 PM

A Grand Union brings together works by students across Sculpture department courses, exploring metal, animation, fictional worlds, and collaborative play.

Organized by Lili Chin, Lecturer in Sculpture, and Nathan Carter, Critic in Undergraduate Studies.
