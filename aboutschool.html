About the School

The Yale School of Art is a graduate school offering Master of Fine Arts degrees in Graphic Design, Painting/Printmaking, Photography, and Sculpture.

The school’s philosophy is rooted in critical dialogue, independence of thought, and the development of a strong personal artistic voice.

Artists and designers of unusual promise and strong motivation are provided an educational context in which they can explore the potential of their talents through intense critical dialogue.

The graduate student’s primary educational experience at Yale is centered on studio activity, supported by rigorous coursework and exposure to diverse aesthetic positions.

The School of Art emphasizes both the refinement of visual skills and the cultivation of the mind, encouraging independence, imagination, and critical thought.

Master of Fine Arts Degree

The Master of Fine Arts is the only degree offered by the School of Art. It is awarded after successful completion of all required coursework and an approved thesis presentation.

The program requires a minimum residence of two years, with student work reviewed by faculty at the end of each term.

Public Lectures & Exhibitions

Each department hosts visiting artists and designers who participate in lectures and critiques.

The School’s galleries provide a year-round forum for exhibitions by students, faculty, and invited guests.
